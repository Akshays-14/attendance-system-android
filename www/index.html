Get a Gemini API key

  1. Tap Open Google AI Studio below and sign in with your Google account.
  2. Tap Create API key (choose or create a project if asked).
  3. Copy the key that appears (it starts with AIza…).
  4. Come back here, paste it into Gemini API key, and tap Save key. The app checks it right away.
  5. Open ERP, log in, go to attendance, and tap ✨ AI Analyse.

Google offers a free tier that is plenty for occasional syncs, but limits can change. Never share your key; anyone who has it can use your quota.
